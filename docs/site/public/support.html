Write to this address for help, bug reports and data-deletion requests. This is the support contact for GAIN: Workout Tracker (shown as GAIN on your home screen).

Report a problem

Please send: what you were doing, what you expected and what happened, your phone make/model and iOS or Android version, the GAIN version (Settings, bottom of the page), and a screenshot if you can. For a crash: Settings > Diagnostics and crash log > Send feedback (it never includes your sets, weights or notes). Never send your backup file or recovery code in a bug report.

Bugs can also be filed publicly at github.com/ElMolla10/gain/issues (no workout data, please).

If you are injured or in pain, this is not the place: stop and ask a qualified professional.
