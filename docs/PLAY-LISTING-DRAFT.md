## English

**App name (max 30):** `GAIN: Next Weight, Lifting Log` (30)

**Short description (max 80):** `Log your lifts. Leave knowing the next weight and if your goal is on pace.` (74)

**Full description:**

> GAIN is a lifting log for people who already train. Finish a workout and GAIN writes the next one for you: the exercise, the weight, the reps, and the reason, using the weights that actually exist in your gym.
>
> WHAT IT DOES
> • Next session, written at the door: after you finish, you see the next targets before you leave. Accept, edit or reject each one. Rejected suggestions are remembered.
> • Your gym's weights: tell GAIN which dumbbells, plates and machine steps your gym has, and the targets use only those.
> • Why this weight: every target shows its reason and the numbers behind it. Open the decision log any time.
> • Is my goal on pace? Name a lift, a bodyweight or a muscle goal. GAIN compares your logged progress with your date and says so in one line. It is an estimate from your own logs, not a promise.
> • Short week? Tell GAIN how many days and minutes you have. It shows what it would cut before changing anything, and can undo it.
> • Holds and carries: plank, dead hang and farmer's walk are logged in seconds or metres.
> • Warm-up sets from today's target, a rest timer, supersets, notes, drop sets.
> • History and trends for each lift. Import your history from Hevy or Strong. Export everything (JSON, CSV) at any time.
> • English and Arabic, with right-to-left layout. kg or lb. Light and dark.
>
> YOUR DATA
> Everything is stored on your phone. There is no account and no ads. Back up and sync to a GAIN server is optional and off until you turn it on. You can delete everything from Settings.
>
> GOOD TO KNOW
> GAIN is a training log and suggestion tool. It is not a doctor, physiotherapist or coach, and it does not promise any result. If something hurts, stop and ask a qualified professional. Suggestions are based only on what you log.

## Known blockers before any submission
1. **Self-update installer.** The app declares `REQUEST_INSTALL_PACKAGES` and downloads its own APK from GitHub. Play distribution generally does not allow an app to update itself outside Play, and a declared install-packages permission needs a policy exemption. The Play build would need this removed (a build flavour without the updater). Not done; needs a decision (D-number to be assigned) and a check of the current policy text.
2. Account, identity checks and testing-track requirements for new developer accounts: **unknown/changing**, check at the time (Step 25).
3. Package id `app.gain.mobile` is permanent once published.
4. Arabic copy needs native review; privacy policy needs legal review and a public URL.
5. Billing: no billing exists. The listing must not mention subscriptions until Step 24 is real.
6. Screenshots need a device or emulator session.
