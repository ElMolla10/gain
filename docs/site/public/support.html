Email: [support email not set yet]

The support address is not set yet: it is a decision for the person who runs GAIN (and the same address is needed for the Google Play listing). Until it is set, pilot lifters use the contact they were given.

Report a problem

Please send: what you were doing, what you expected and what happened, your phone make/model and Android version, the GAIN version (Settings, bottom of the page), and a screenshot if you can. For a crash: Settings > Diagnostics and crash log > Send feedback (it never includes your sets, weights or notes). Never send your backup file or recovery code in a bug report.

Bugs can also be filed publicly at github.com/ElMolla10/gain/issues (no workout data, please).

If you are injured or in pain, this is not the place: stop and ask a qualified professional.
