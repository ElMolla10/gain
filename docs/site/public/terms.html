GAIN terms of use

Last updated 2026-10-27. العربية ↓

  1. What GAIN is. A strength-training log that suggests next-session loads from your own history. A tool, not medical, coaching, nutrition or physiotherapy advice.
  2. Your responsibility. You decide every load and whether to train. Check with a qualified professional if you are injured, in pain, pregnant, unwell, or returning after a break. Use safe technique, spotters and equipment.
  3. Suggestions can be wrong. Targets, pace and weekly decisions are estimates from what you logged (and from default rules). They are not promises of results; GAIN makes no physique, weight or performance promises.
  4. Your data. It stays on your phone unless you turn on the optional backup (privacy policy). You can export and delete it. Back it up yourself: a lost phone without a backup loses the data.
  5. Test builds. Test builds (TestFlight or a sideloaded APK) come as they are, may contain bugs, and may change. Updates are installed only by you.
  6. Content. Exercise names, Arabic names and aliases are provided as they are and may contain mistakes. The iPhone app is also covered by Apple's standard licensed-application end-user agreement.
  7. Free at launch. GAIN currently has no subscriptions, in-app purchases or paid feature tiers, so there are no fees or refunds for the current version.
  8. Liability. GAIN is provided as it is. To the extent the law allows, the operator is not liable for injury, loss or damage that comes from using the app or following a suggestion; nothing here limits liability that cannot be limited by law.
  9. Age. You must be at least 13 to use GAIN; see the privacy policy.
  10. Governing law. Egyptian law governs these terms, and disputes go to the competent courts in Egypt, without taking away consumer rights that apply where you live.
