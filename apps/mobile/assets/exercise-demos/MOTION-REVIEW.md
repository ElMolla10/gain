# Static motion-arrow review

All 45 bundled illustrations and their stable seed-key mappings were inspected before annotation. 42 receive an individually placed static SVG overlay; three remain unchanged. Arrows show a movement from the pictured pose, so a raised bench bar points down while a shoulder-height overhead press points up. They indicate direction, not a prescribed speed, range, weight, or logging action.

The original CC0 WebPs, the source link, and licensing/attribution in README.md are preserved byte-for-byte. The local paths share the image's contain fitting, have neon brand strokes with a dark outline, and remain in image coordinates in English and Arabic. No network fetch or animation is used in the app. Upstream end poses were consulted as review references only and are not bundled or fetched by the app.
